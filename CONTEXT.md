# Pillout domain context

Pillout is a mobile-first match-three capsule game with a React frontend and a Python FastAPI service in one monorepo. This file records the current domain language for contributors and engineering skills.

## Game language

- **Tile / pill:** a uniquely identified board object. Each medicine has its own match kind, named by its medicine ID. The three fruit kinds are mint (apple), gold (strawberry), and lavender (peach).
- **Tile pool:** the medicines selected in player setup plus all three fruits. Unselected medicines never appear on a generated board or in refills and shuffles. A quick game with no medicine selection uses fruits only. Changing the medicine selection starts a fresh round immediately, removing deselected medicines and cancelling any pending animation from the previous round.
- **Special:** an earned bomb, hammer, or crown. Specials do not match by their hidden base kind; swap with any neighbor to activate. Four in a straight line earns a hammer, a T/L intersection earns a bomb, and five or more in a straight line earns a crown. Each connected match group earns one special, with crown taking priority over bomb and hammer.
- **Bomb:** clears a 3-by-3 area centered on its position after the swap, clipped at the board edge.
- **Hammer:** clears a full row for a horizontal swap or column for a vertical swap. A chained hammer uses the direction of the match that created it.
- **Crown:** clears all regular tiles of its swap partner’s kind. When chained or swapped with another special, targets the most common regular kind (ties follow `KINDS`). Swapping two crowns clears the whole board.
- **Special chain:** a blast triggers specials it hits, each at most once per wave. Overlapping effects count a position once. A newly earned special survives the wave that created it and falls with gravity.
- **Board:** an 8-by-8 grid of pills, stored in row-major order.
- **Swap:** exchanging horizontally or vertically adjacent pills. Diagonal swaps and row wrapping are invalid.
- **Match:** a horizontal or vertical run of at least three pills of the same kind. Intersecting runs clear each position once.
- **Cascade:** another match produced after a clear, gravity, and refill. Each cascade increases the score multiplier within that move.
- **Move:** a successful swap that creates a match or activates a special. Invalid swaps revert without consuming a move.
- **Classic:** a saved level adventure mixing score goals and bear-rescue goals, with 22–26 base moves. Ten-level chapters alternate steady play, challenges, and breathers. Score goals compensate for the tile pool and grow between chapters with a bounded ceiling. Reaching the goal on the last move is a win. See [level difficulty](docs/level-difficulty.md) for research and tuning.
- **Retry help:** every two completed losses add two moves to the next attempt, capped at six. The UI announces help; goals and tile odds stay fixed during an attempt. Winning unlocks the next level and resets losses. Restarting and abandoning a round do not count as losses.
- **Zen:** no move limit and no terminal score.
- **Bear rescue:** a level goal in which each bear is collected once its covering has been fully cleared. Rescuing all required bears within the move limit replaces the score requirement; score remains a bonus and does not determine victory.
- **Classic goal variety:** Classic mixes score-goal and bear-rescue levels in the same saved progression. Stages 1, 4, and 8 of each ten-level chapter require 1, 2, and 3 bears respectively, repeating the same counts each chapter; the remaining stages use score goals.
- **Bear footprint:** the fixed 2-by-2 area occupied by one bear beneath normal playable pills, without overlapping another bear. All four cells must be uncovered to rescue that bear; the bear stays in place as pills fall, refill, and shuffle.
- **Rescue layout:** the bear locations assigned to a level, unchanged across retries of that level. Pills remain random on each attempt.
- **Covering:** the fixed, single removable layer covering the whole board beneath normal playable pills at the start of a bear-rescue attempt. The first clear on a covered cell through a match, cascade, or special effect permanently uncovers that cell; adjacent matches alone do not uncover it, shuffles preserve discoveries, and covering outside bear footprints may remain when the level is won.
- **Special creation and covering:** every cell in a match uncovers, including the cell converted into an earned special. The new special survives normally.
- **Hint:** a legal regular or special swap highlighted on the board. Hints are free.
- **Shuffle:** fresh regular tiles with no opening matches and at least one legal move; earned specials stay at their positions. Shuffles are free; dead boards are also replaced automatically.
- **Session:** the active round. A session identifier prevents unfinished asynchronous work from modifying a restarted round.

## Scoring and lifecycle

Each cleared tile or tile converted into an earned special earns 50 points multiplied by the current cascade number. A special activation, including its full chain and resulting cascades, consumes exactly one Classic move. A session resolves a swap through `swapping`, `clearing`, and `falling` phases before returning to `idle`. Manual and automatic shuffles use a `shuffling` phase for 480ms (120ms with reduced motion), keeping earned specials at their positions. User moves are blocked while resolving, shuffling, paused, or after a Classic result.

Preferences, personal best, the next unlocked level, and its capped loss count persist on the current device. A win saves the unlock before the player continues. Active rounds stay in memory across route navigation; refreshing starts a fresh attempt at the saved level. The API exposes health and database-backed operations for users, drugs, avatars, and their associations. Player setup loads medicine and avatar catalogs from the API. Submitting Welcome saves the player profile and selected avatar, then reads the saved profile back into setup. Later submissions update that same player during the current browser session. Drafts and the player ID stay in memory, with no automatic profile recovery after refresh or authentication. Patient details, medicine selections, schedules, and active game state remain local and are not sent to the API.

## Architecture

Daily rewards grant one claim for the first Classic level won on each local calendar day. Zen, losses, and abandoned attempts do not earn rewards. Missed days preserve the seven-day track, and unclaimed days remain available. Caregivers save a selection of preset or custom prizes; each claim snapshots one selected prize into the child's prize pocket, where it can be marked received. Choices must be saved before the first claim. Rewards, prize choices, and received status persist only on this device, without API storage or authentication. Milestones at 8, 15, 22, and 30 days celebrate total reward days; they do not grant extra prizes.

- `apps/web/src/features/DailyRewards/`: reward rules, persisted Zustand state, seven-day calendar, prize claims, and caregiver choices. The game store records rewards at the resolved Classic win boundary. `/rewards` and `/reward-choices` expose the feature.
- Reward screens fit the available mobile viewport without page or nested scrolling. Caregiver choices paginate six prizes at a time, claims paginate four options, and the prize pocket shows one saved prize at a time. Custom-prize entry replaces the chooser while editing and follows the visual viewport height so its controls remain visible above the on-screen keyboard.

- `apps/web/src/game/engine.ts`: pure level configuration, board generation, adjacency, matching, legal moves, swaps, and gravity/refill. Generation, refill, and shuffle accept the session's tile pool; `game/medicines.ts` defines the available medicine IDs.
- `apps/web/src/game/store.ts`: Zustand session lifecycle, async resolution, score, outcome, saved level progression, retry help, preferences, and persistence.
- `apps/web/src/components/game-board.tsx`: Pixi application lifecycle, capsule presentation, interpolation, and the semantic HTML input grid.
- `apps/web/src/game/effects.ts`: transient clear-wave and shuffle event contracts and motion timing. Engine resolution supplies actual special origins, axes, and affected cells; effects never infer a second set of rules. Each clear wave retains the initiating pointer or keyboard input for presentation decisions across cascades.
- `apps/web/src/components/game-board-effects.ts`: Pixi special blasts, earned-special halos, cascade particles, and shuffle presentation. `features/GameEffects/` presents cascade lettering, score comets, and the avatar/trophy victory sequence. Both system and in-app reduced motion replace travel with stationary feedback; sound remains opt-in.
- `apps/web/src/pages/game-page.tsx`: illustrated game screen, settings, stats, and result UI. Selected medicines use the same sprites as the medicine picker; fruits and earned bombs, hammers, and crowns have dedicated artwork. The goal and move budget follow the current level configuration. Bear-rescue levels use the supplied bear artwork and a rescued/required counter; score is a bonus on these levels. Limited toolbar boosters are not game mechanics.
- `apps/web/src/App.tsx`: app shell and typed TanStack routes: `/` (intro), `/welcome` (player), `/pills` (selection), `/plan` (times and quantities), `/play` (game), and `/how-to-play`.
- `apps/web/src/features/BearRescue/`: fixed bear and covering artwork beneath the Pixi pills, plus the goal counter and a brief icon response to interim pointer-driven rescues. The counter updates immediately; keyboard input skips the new accent and reduced motion uses opacity only. Presentation observes the engine-supplied rescue state; it does not resolve discoveries.
- `apps/web/src/features/PlayerSetup/`: player, avatar, pill selection, and demo schedule UI. `api.ts` maps API catalog IDs to supported sprite keys and converts saved player fields. Its Zustand store manages form drafts, catalog loading, explicit profile saves, and retry state, and updates the game session's medicine pool when the selection changes. Only the player profile and selected avatar are saved; patient details and schedules remain a local demo, without authentication, medical records, or reminders.
- `apps/web/src/lib/api.ts`: shared HTTP client using relative `/api` requests, timeouts, and request error handling.
- `apps/web/public/art/`: supplied logos, board/title artwork, design sheets, transparent avatar and pill atlases, fruits, and special-piece assets. `lib/art-assets.ts` shares pixel frames between HTML and Pixi. `components/artwork.tsx` displays atlas regions; `features/PlayerSetup/components/Avatar.tsx` renders the same avatar sprites in setup and gameplay. Pixi loads the pill atlas and individual fruit textures. Bomb, hammer, and crown rules live in the engine, and their visual metadata lives in `lib/art-assets.ts`.
- `apps/web/src/components/ui/`: owned shadcn Base UI components.
- `apps/web/src/styles/app.css` and `apps/web/src/index.css`: mobile-first layouts, theme tokens, and styling.
- `apps/api/src/pillout_api/main.py`: framework-native ASGI application, `/api/health`, and user, drug, and avatar operations. Users reference their selected avatar with `avatar_id`; legacy `avatar_filename` writes remain supported. `database.py` configures the Turso/libSQL client, applies `apps/api/schema.sql`, and upgrades older tables at startup. `catalog.py` seeds built-in medicines and avatars idempotently with unique `catalog_key` values matching frontend sprite identities. Unkeyed legacy catalog rows are retained but are not shown by the sprite-based pickers.
- `apps/api/pyproject.toml` and `apps/api/uv.lock`: independent Python dependencies, packaging, and quality tools.
- Root `package.json` and `pnpm-workspace.yaml`: pnpm task orchestration for both apps. The API package wrapper calls uv; it does not manage Python dependencies.

The web dev server proxies `/api` to the local API on port 8000. The web and API can be built and run independently. Root `vercel.json` deploys both through Vercel Services on one domain: `/api/*` and the API documentation routes go to FastAPI; other paths go to the Vite SPA with a deep-link fallback. The API reads `TURSO_DATABASE_URL` and `TURSO_AUTH_TOKEN` supplied by the project's Vercel Turso integration. Player profiles and selected avatars are saved through the API; patient plans and gameplay remain local to the browser.

The engine remains independent of React, Pixi, and Zustand. Presentation consumes session state rather than implementing another rules engine. New features should respect these boundaries while following the feature-folder guidance where applicable.
